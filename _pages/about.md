I'm an MSc student in Computer Science, majoring in Machine Intelligence, at ETH Zürich. Before that, I completed my Bachelor's in Engineering of Computer Systems at Politecnico di Milano (110/110 with honors), with an exchange year at Universidad de Málaga.

I'm currently turning the protein language model ESM2 into a generative model for antibody design via Direct Preference Optimization, with the [Learning & Adaptive Systems Group](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/) (Prof. Andreas Krause). With the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch/) Apertus group, I trained linear probes on LLM hidden states that warn, token by token, before a model falls into a repetitive loop; this work, _Degeneration Detection Is Not Early Warning_, was accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Interpretability as a Science, and I also contributed to the [Apertus v1.5 technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15542) as part of the Interpretability team. Earlier this year, with the [Computer Vision and Geometry Lab](https://cvg.ethz.ch/) (Daniel Barath), I trained a reinforcement learning agent to dynamically allocate resolution in 3D maps under a tight memory budget.

I'm broadly interested in machine learning for the natural sciences, language model interpretability and safety, and reinforcement learning. You can find more details on my [publications](/publications/), [projects](/projects/) and [resume](/cv/) pages.

Feel free to [reach out](mailto:mdenegri@ethz.ch) if you'd like to chat about research, collaborate, or just say hi.
